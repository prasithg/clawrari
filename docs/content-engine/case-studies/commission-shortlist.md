# Case Studies to Commission — Shortlist

First 3 case studies to commission for Clawrari, with outreach playbook. Profiles are categories — actual candidates come from GitHub stars/issues/forks once the project has traffic.

---

## Case Study A: The Solo Technical Founder

**Profile:** Technical founder (1-3 person startup) using Claude Code or similar daily, currently struggling with context loss across sessions and inconsistent agent behavior.

**Why this matters:** This is Clawrari's core user. If it doesn't work for a solo founder running agents as infrastructure, the project has no legs.

**What we'd document:**
- Their setup before Clawrari (what was breaking)
- Bootstrap experience (time to first useful session)
- Specific patterns they adopted and what changed
- Honest friction points

**How to find them:** Watch GitHub stars + issues. The person who files a thoughtful issue within 48 hours of starring is the right candidate.

## Case Study B: The AI Engineer with a Team

**Profile:** AI/ML engineer at a 10-50 person company who manages agent workflows for a small team. Cares about reproducibility and onboarding — not just personal productivity.

**Why this matters:** Proves Clawrari scales beyond one person. The "team" use case (shared conventions, onboarding new agents to a codebase) is where OSS projects get real traction.

**What we'd document:**
- How they adapted Clawrari conventions for team use
- What they changed vs. kept from defaults
- Onboarding time for a new team member's agent setup
- What's still missing for team workflows

**How to find them:** Clawrari Discord (once live) or Twitter/X replies to build-in-public posts. The person asking "how do I share this across my team?" is the signal.

## Case Study C: The Power User Who Forked and Modified

**Profile:** Developer who cloned Clawrari, ripped out parts they didn't need, added their own patterns, and is running a heavily customized version.

**Why this matters:** Shows the project is composable, not monolithic. The best OSS projects get forked and remixed. This case study proves the architecture supports that.

**What we'd document:**
- What they kept, what they removed, what they added
- Why they forked instead of just configuring
- What upstream changes they'd want back
- Their custom patterns (potential upstream contributions)

**How to find them:** GitHub forks list. Anyone with >5 commits on their fork within 30 days of forking is worth reaching out to.

## Outreach Script

> Hey {name} — I'm Prasith, I built Clawrari. Saw you {specific_signal: filed an issue / forked and made changes / asked about team workflows}.
>
> I'm writing up a few short case studies on how people are actually using it. Not a puff piece — I want to know what works, what's broken, and what you'd change.
>
> Would you be up for a 15-min async chat (or a short call) about your setup? I'll share the draft before anything goes public and you can kill it if it doesn't feel right.
>
> No pressure either way.
