# Intro Templates

Three short outreach templates in Prasith's voice for first contact with adjacent OSS maintainers. Each ≤180 words. No hype, no "I hope this finds you well," no closing flourishes. Sign-off is `— Prasith` only.

**Rules of use:**

- These are drafts. Personalize every placeholder. Never send a template with `{...}` still in it.
- Prasith reviews and approves every message before send.
- One template per outreach — do not stack asks. If A is "want to chat," do not also pitch a demo and a cross-post.
- If you cannot fill `{specific_thing_we_admire}` with something real after reading their work, do not send. Generic praise reads worse than no praise.

## Template A — Peer collab

> Subject: Clawrari + {their_project}
>
> Hey {maintainer_name},
>

>
> The overlap I see: {our_overlap_thesis}. Different scopes, same instincts.
>
> Not pitching anything. Wanted to introduce myself in case it's useful to know who's working in the neighborhood. {ask}
>
> Repo: https://github.com/prasithg/clawrari
>
> — Prasith

## Placeholder cheat-sheet

| Placeholder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `{maintainer_name}` | First name only. Use the name they use publicly. |
| `{their_project}` | Project name as they write it (case + spacing matters). |
| `{specific_thing_we_admire}` | One concrete observation from their repo / blog / talk. Must be specific enough that they could not mistake it for boilerplate. Bad: "love the work." Good: "the way `superpowers/` keeps skill frontmatter minimal and human-readable." |
| `{our_overlap_thesis}` | One sentence on the *intersection* — not what we do, not what they do, but the slice where we both live. |
| `{ask}` | Exactly one ask. "Worth a 20-minute call?" or "Open to a PR adding X?" or "Want to swap drafts before publish?" Never two. |
