# learnings.md — the compounding layer

> TEMPLATE. Append-only. Gitignore your filled copy. This is what turns a one-off
> chatbot into an advisor that gets better every time. After each session, add any
> durable correction, preference, or outcome. NEVER rewrite past entries — the
> point is an honest record of what you learned and when.

## How to use

- **Append, don't edit.** Each entry is dated. History is the value.
- **Capture three kinds of thing:**
  1. **Corrections** — "stop recommending X," "I actually meant Y."
  2. **Preferences** — how you like to decide, what you value that wasn't obvious.
  3. **Outcomes** — a decision you made and how it turned out (the advisor learns from your real track record, not just your stated goals).
- **Promote stable patterns to `plan.md`** when a learning becomes a permanent fact about you.

## Entry format

```
### <YYYY-MM-DD> — <short title>
**Type:** correction | preference | outcome
**What:** <one or two sentences>
**Why it matters for advice:** <how a future answer should change>
```
