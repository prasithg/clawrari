Philosophy

Opinionated defaults. Not 47 options. THE setup, with rationale for every choice. Fork and customize from a known-good baseline.

Battle-tested. Everything here runs in production daily. If it's in Clawrari, it works. If it doesn't work, it's not in Clawrari.

Your AI should have a personality. Not a corporate drone. Not a sycophant. A sharp partner with opinions, humor, and honest feedback.

Memory is the foundation. An AI that forgets everything between sessions is just a search engine. Structured memory is what makes it a second brain.

Errors are bugs. If a cron job fails, that's not a footnote. That's something to fix now. Silence around failures is the enemy.
